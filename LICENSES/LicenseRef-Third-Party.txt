Third-party documents

The files of corpus/ (the originals and their readable copies in corpus/text/) and the PDF file of
inbox/ are official documents published by the bodies named in each of them: European Union
institutions, French, Catalan, Flemish and Italian public bodies. They are kept here as they were
retrieved, so that the workshops can use real documents.

They belong to their publishers and keep their own terms. The licences of this repository do not
cover them, and neither do they cover the photographs, logos or emblems these documents contain.
Before you reuse one, read the terms of its source: the address is in its header.
