# Italy (Naples): what to do in case of a forest fire

If you see flames, or even only smoke, call 115, the number of the national fire brigade.
Where it is active, you can call the single emergency number 112.
Do not assume that someone else has already called.
Give the information needed to locate the fire.
Look for a safe escape route, such as a road or a watercourse.
Do not stop in places towards which the wind is blowing.
Lie down on the ground in a place without burnable vegetation.
Smoke rises, so this helps you avoid breathing it.
If you have no other choice, cross the fire where it is least intense, to reach the already burnt side.
A fire is not a show: do not stop along roads.
To prevent fires, do not throw lit cigarettes or matches.
Do not light fires in the woods; use only equipped areas.
Do not park a car with its hot exhaust touching dry grass.
Do not burn stubble, straw or farm residues without proper safety measures.
