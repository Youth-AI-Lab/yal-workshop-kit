# The 2025 wildfire season in the EU (EU civil protection summary)

The EU had its most devastating wildfire season on record in 2025.
Over 1 million hectares burnt, according to EFFIS, the European Forest Fire Information System.
EFFIS satellite monitoring counted 7 783 wildfires in 25 EU countries.
More than 100 000 hectares had already burnt by the end of March.
In August, a long heatwave caused 22 major fires in Portugal and Spain.
These fires burnt 460 585 hectares, which the page calls "nearly half" of the EU's total burnt area.
The page says that beyond the EU, wildfires burnt 2.2 million hectares across Europe, the Middle East and North Africa.
Ukraine accounted for 30% of the total burnt area and 39% of all fires tracked by EFFIS.
On 25 March 2026, the European Commission adopted a new strategy on the rising wildfire threat.
