# The 2025 wildfire season in the EU (EFFIS figures)

The 2025 fire season was the worst on record for the European Union.
The figures come from satellite analysis by EFFIS, the European Forest Fire Information System, managed by the JRC.
In 2025, 1 079 538 hectares burnt in the EU.
EFFIS mapped 7 783 fires in 25 of the 27 EU Member States (all except Luxembourg and Malta).
This is the highest burnt area in the EU27 recorded by EFFIS since 2006.
It is nearly double the 2006–2024 average.
Germany, Spain, Cyprus and Slovakia hit all-time records.
In the first three weeks of August, a heatwave triggered 22 very large fires in Portugal and Spain.
These fires burnt 460 585 hectares, 43% of the EU total.
About 39% of the EU burnt area was inside Natura 2000 protected sites (424 023 hectares).
Across all territories monitored by EFFIS (Europe, Middle East, North Africa, EU included), 2 242 195 hectares burnt.
Of these, 1 092 095 hectares were in non-EU European countries and 70 562 in the Middle East and North Africa.
Outside the EU, Ukraine was the most affected country in the area monitored by EFFIS: almost 30% of the total mapped burnt area and 39% of all mapped fires.
