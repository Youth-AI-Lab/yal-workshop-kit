# Bouches-du-Rhône (Aix-en-Provence): summer access to forest massifs

The prefecture says Bouches-du-Rhône is one of the départements most exposed to forest fire in mainland France.
There are about 250 fire starts per year in the département.
They burn about 1 900 hectares per year.
Nearly 90% of fire starts are of human origin.
From 1 June to 30 September inclusive, access to, presence in and movement in forest massifs are regulated.
This rule comes from a prefectoral order of 22 April 2025.
The order was partly revised on 9 July 2026.
During this period, access to each massif is decided every day, based on weather and drought.
The decision is published on a map at about 6 pm for the next day.
Works that can start a fire are also regulated during this period.
Examples are brush clearing, farm work and fuel-powered machinery (engins thermiques).
Works can be allowed under conditions, restricted, or forbidden when the risk is very severe or extreme.
Public events and professional audiovisual shoots in massifs from June to September must be declared to the mayor, the DDTM and the fire and rescue service at least two months in advance.
