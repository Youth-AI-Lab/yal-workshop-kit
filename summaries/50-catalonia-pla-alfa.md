# Catalonia (Barcelona): the Pla Alfa fire danger levels

The Pla Alfa is the operational procedure of the Agents Rurals, Catalonia's rural agents corps, for forest-fire danger.
It applies all year.
It rates the danger every day on 5 levels.
Level 0 is low danger, 1 moderate, 2 high, 3 very high and 4 extreme.
All levels apply per municipality.
The Pla Alfa map is updated at 00:00 and 9:30, or at other times if needed.
Before any activity with a fire risk, it must be notified to, or authorised by, the Catalan Department of Agriculture, Livestock, Fisheries and Food.
Notification applies from 16 October to 14 March.
Authorisation applies from 15 March to 15 October.
In both cases, the Agents Rurals enforce the measures of Decree 64/95 on forest-fire prevention.
At level 3, all fire-risk activities already authorised or notified are suspended.
At level 3, lighting a fire in any open space is forbidden, including road rest areas, recreation and camping areas, even the places equipped for fires.
At level 3, bringing in and using pyrotechnic material is forbidden.
At level 4, access to protected natural areas and busy forest areas can be restricted, except for residents and essential services.
The emergency number is 112.
