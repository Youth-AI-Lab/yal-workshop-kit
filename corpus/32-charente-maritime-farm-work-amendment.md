# Charente-Maritime (La Rochelle): farm work and the June 2026 amending order
Source: Préfecture de la Charente-Maritime, page "Prévention des incendies de forêt, d'espaces naturels et agricoles", https://www.charente-maritime.gouv.fr/Actions-de-l-Etat/Securite/Securite-civile/Prevention-des-incendies-de-foret-d-espaces-naturels-et-agricoles, consulted 19 October 2026 (amending order dated 24/06/2026), French
Last checked: 2026-10-20 by Claude (draft — needs human verification)

Prefectoral order n° 26EB443 of 24 June 2026 amends the order on fire use in Charente-Maritime (n° 26EB142).
It clarifies which farm work is covered by the restrictions and bans on work that can start a fire.
It excludes field-crop harvests, haymaking, mowing and baling from these restrictions and bans.
