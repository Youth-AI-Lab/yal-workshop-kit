# Campania: the 2026 period of serious fire danger
Source: Città di Montoro (Province of Avellino, Campania), Ordinanza n. 78 del 12/06/2026 "Applicazione delle misure di prevenzione del rischio incendi boschivi in vista del periodo di grave pericolosità dichiarato per l'anno 2026", https://montoro-api.municipiumapp.it/s3/20111/allegati/uffici-e-servizi/settore-vigilianza-comando-polizia-locale-e-protezione-civile/ordinanza-n-78-del-12-06-2026.pdf, 12 June 2026, Italian. Municipal ordinance quoting regional rules; the regional decree itself was not read.

The Campania Region declared a state of serious forest-fire danger for 2026, by decree n. 212 of 9 June 2026.
According to this ordinance, it applied to the whole region from 15 June 2026 to 30 September 2026.
During this period, burning farm and forest plant residues is forbidden.
Burning stubble and weeds, even on uncultivated land, is forbidden from 1 June to 20 September (regional law 26/2012).
Lighting fires outdoors in woods, and up to 100 metres from them, is forbidden.
Lighting fires in pastures is also forbidden.
In woods and pastures, smoking and throwing lit matches or cigarettes are forbidden.
The ordinance forbids fireworks, rockets and sky lanterns "a una distanza non inferiore a 1 km dalle superfici boscate e pascoli" (literally: "at a distance not less than 1 km from woods and pastures").
The mayor can allow exceptions for public events.
Campsites, hotels and holiday facilities exposed to fire must clear vegetation within at least 20 metres.
