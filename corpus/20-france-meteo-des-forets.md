# France: the "Météo des forêts" fire danger forecast
Source: Météo-France, page "Météo des forêts", https://meteofrance.com/meteo-des-forets, no update date on the page (linked article dated 23/07/2026), French
Last checked: 2026-10-19 by Claude (draft — needs human verification)

Météo-France has published the Météo des forêts since 2023.
It tells the public every day the forest-fire danger level.
The level is based on weather forecasts and on how dry the vegetation is.
It is given per département, for the next day and the day after.
It has 4 levels: low (faible, green), moderate (modéré, yellow), high (élevé, orange) and very high (très élevé, red).
The very high, high and moderate levels compare the risk with normal summer conditions.
At the low level, the forecast weather and recent rain reduce the risk.
The Météo des forêts does not report fires in progress or upcoming fires.
A low level does not mean there is no fire risk in the département.
Mayors and prefectures can restrict access to exposed areas and some activities.
People should check their prefecture's website.
When checked on 19 October 2026, the page said the forest-fire season in mainland France was over.
It said the Météo des forêts would come back in June 2027.
