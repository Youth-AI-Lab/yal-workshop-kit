# Charente-Maritime (La Rochelle): what fire use is forbidden
Source: Préfecture de la Charente-Maritime, Arrêté préfectoral n° 26EB142 "réglementant l'usage du feu à l'air libre en vue de prévenir les incendies de forêt et d'espaces naturels dans le département de la Charente-Maritime", https://www.charente-maritime.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/85472/601661/file/AP26EB142-Usages_du_feu_2026_04_30.pdf, signed 30 April 2026, in force 1 May 2026, French (scanned PDF, text read by OCR)
Last checked: 2026-10-19 by Claude (draft — needs human verification)

Burning waste outdoors or in individual incinerators is forbidden all year in all of Charente-Maritime.
This includes green waste from private people, professionals and local authorities.
Private people cannot get an exemption to burn green waste.
Smoking is forbidden in all the forest areas defined by the order (articles 2.5 and 2.6).
Releasing sky lanterns is strictly forbidden in the whole département, at every vigilance level.
In these forest areas, cooking fires on the ground are forbidden.
Electric and gas barbecues, planchas and electric stoves are not covered by the order.
From level SÉVÈRE (orange), fireworks and pyrotechnic shows are forbidden, except at sea or from the foreshore of the beaches of the coast and of the Gironde estuary, under conditions (safety perimeter, 200-metre buffer zone).
From level TRÈS SÉVÈRE (red), all fireworks are forbidden.
In these forest areas, using equipment that can start a fire by heat or sparks (for example hot work, thermal weeding, farm work, earthworks, forestry work, cutting, welding, grinding) is forbidden from 1 pm to midnight from level SÉVÈRE (orange).
It is forbidden all day from level TRÈS SÉVÈRE (red).
An amending order of 24 June 2026 changed the rules for farm work: see 32-charente-maritime-farm-work-amendment.md.
Breaking the order is punished by a 1st-class fine.
The forestry code also provides a 4th-class fine for some offences.
