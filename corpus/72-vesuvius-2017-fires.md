# Campania (Naples): the 2017 fires in the Vesuvius National Park
Source: Ente Parco Nazionale del Vesuvio, document "PNV-29-gen-slides" (Word file on the park's website), https://www.parconazionaledelvesuvio.it/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/PNV-29-gen-slides.docx, uploaded January 2019 according to the URL (no date inside the document), Italian
Last checked: 2026-10-19 by Claude (draft — needs human verification)

In July and August 2017, a series of fires burnt inside the Vesuvius National Park.
The fires affected 3 194 hectares.
This is about 80% of the park's total wooded area.
By severity, 374 hectares were burnt at high severity.
2 147 hectares were burnt at medium-high severity.
466 hectares were burnt at medium-low severity.
207 hectares were burnt at low severity.
Many planted conifers died.
The document (uploaded in January 2019) expects fuel to build up over the following 10 years.
Without proper management, this could help new fires start and spread over large areas.
Conifer stands burnt at high and medium-high severity are the most exposed to erosion.
650 hectares were identified as high priority for intervention, including 300 hectares for primary work.
When the document was written, two pilot restoration projects were being examined.
They cover about 80 hectares of pine forest in four Vesuvius municipalities: Terzigno, Boscotrecase, Trecase and Ottaviano.
Their total amount is 1 200 000 euros.
