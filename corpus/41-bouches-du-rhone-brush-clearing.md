# Bouches-du-Rhône (Aix-en-Provence): brush-clearing rules (OLD)
Source: Préfecture des Bouches-du-Rhône (Service Régional de la Communication Interministérielle), press kit "Les obligations légales de débroussaillement (O.L.D.) dans les Bouches-du-Rhône", https://www.bouches-du-rhone.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/62204/437861/file/DP%20-%20OLD%20nouvel%20arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%2011.2025-def.pdf, November 2025, French
Last checked: 2026-10-20 by Sébastien Nedjar

Forest covers about one third of the Bouches-du-Rhône, about 175 000 hectares.
From 1975 to 2024, more than 228 fires a year on average threatened the forest massifs.
On average, 1 930 hectares a year were burnt.
A new prefectoral order on brush clearing was signed on 15 October 2025.
The obligation applies to owners of land less than 200 metres from woods and forests.
Owners must clear 50 metres around their house.
They must clear 10 metres on each side of the access roads to their land.
The owner is responsible for clearing around each building; the mayor is first responsible for checking, except for linear networks and municipal property.
The new order protects some nature: no clearing in riparian forests and riverside woodland (ripisylves).
Through ecological exemptions, remarkable trees, groups of trees, islands of vegetation and standing dead trees can be kept.
Clearing is done gradually, from the buildings towards natural areas, so animals can escape to refuge areas.
Not clearing is a 5th-class offence, with a criminal fine of up to 50 € per square metre not cleared.
The press kit says this potential fine can reach 392 500 €.
An administrative fine of up to 50 € per square metre is also possible.
